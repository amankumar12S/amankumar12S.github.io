## Research Profile

I work across computer vision and deep learning, with research spanning medical imaging, remote sensing, space-weather forecasting, and degraded text recognition. My strengths are designing efficient neural architectures, building synthetic data pipelines when real labels are scarce, and testing whether models hold up under distribution shift through cross-domain evaluation and conformal prediction. I am a research intern at ISI Kolkata with Prof. Umapada Pal and at the University of Salford with Dr. Shivakumara Palaiahnakote, and I lead a student research team at SMIT. I am applying to Master's and Ph.D. programs for Fall 2027 to continue this work at a deeper level.

**Research Intern, CVPR Unit — Indian Statistical Institute (ISI), Kolkata**  
*Supervisor: Prof. Umapada Pal | Jun. 2026 – Present (Hybrid)*
